From Achen’s Desk 

Dear Brothers and Sisters,

Greetings to you in the precious name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

As we prepare to welcome November, we see the autumn leaves fall and nature prepares for winter. We understand that every season of life has its own beauty and purpose in God’s hands.

November begins with All Saints’ Day on November 1, a day that celebrates the faith and witness of the people of God across generations. We remember those whose lives reflected Christ’s love through courage, sacrifice, humility, and service. Their witness reminds us that holiness is not limited to extraordinary people or great achievements; it is also revealed in ordinary acts of faithfulness, forgiveness, and compassion.

November 11 is Veterans Day in the United States, a time to honor all who have served in the nation’s armed forces. We express our gratitude for their service and remember the sacrifices made by service members and their families.

November is also Native American Heritage Month in the United States, recognizing the histories, cultures, traditions, and contributions of Indigenous peoples. This observance invites us to appreciate the rich diversity of our nation. Thanksgiving Day falls on Thursday, November 26, in 2026. Families and friends will gather around tables to share food, fellowship, and memories.

As November draws to a close, the Church begins to turn its attention towards Advent, the season of preparation for the celebration of Christ’s birth. Advent reminds us that Christian hope is active, watchful, and expectant. We look back with gratitude to the coming of Jesus into the world, celebrate His presence among us, and look forward in hope to the fulfilment of God’s purposes.

This is a good time for families to begin preparing spiritually for Christmas. Beyond decorations, shopping, and celebrations, let us make room for prayer, Scripture reading, reconciliation, and acts of kindness.

May we enter Advent with hearts ready to welcome Christ anew. Let the coming Christmas season not only brighten our homes but also renew our commitment to bring the light of Christ into our neighborhoods.

With prayers and best wishes for a blessed November and a meaningful beginning to the Advent season.

Rev. Sandeep Jacob
